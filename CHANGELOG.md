A record of how the description has changed, and why. The git history holds every edit; this file holds the *reasons* — the moments the practice taught us something and the document caught up. Keep entries short. Newest on top.

## 2026-06 — Initial description

First working description, seeded from two live pairs and a long process of thinking it through. Establishes the core: the dyad as the irreducible unit, the four commitments (write your own creed; pursue God out loud; hold yourself to it; swap creeds when deep enough), contagion-not-quota spread, and God at the center as the source of durability. This is a v1 to think with and to show a kindred spirit — not canon. The truer version comes after more pairs.
