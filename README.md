# The Call

*A practice of pairs, in pursuit of an unreachable God.*

> **About this document.** This is a *working description*, not a protocol. It is the map of something a few of us are already doing, refined as each of us runs real pairs, and offered to anyone who wants to start their own. **It serves the practice and never substitutes for it.** If working on this document ever competes with the calls themselves, the calls win.

---

## What it is

Two meanings, held together. The pursuit of God's **call** — the orientation toward the one goal that outlives every circumstance. And the phone **call** — the slow, real, voice-to-voice conversation where two people actually do the pursuing, together.

The Call is not an organization, a doctrine, or an app. It is a relationship you form on purpose, and a thing you might one day offer to someone else.

---

## The need

We are living through a hollowing. The institutions that once formed people — that taught a person what a human being is *for*, and trained them slowly toward it — have mostly shed that work. The church traded formation for information; the university traded cultivation for credentials. What is left forms us anyway: the feed, the market, the metric. People are lonelier, more atomized, and more uncertain of any shared answer than any generation we have measured.

The response is not a better argument or a wider audience. It is the oldest equipment a culture forgets it needs until it is gone: a small number of people who know your name, to whom you have made yourself accountable, in pursuit of something worth the trouble.

---

## The unit is a pair

The irreducible unit of The Call is two people. Not a group, not a congregation, not a platform — a **dyad**.

A pair cannot hide. There is no third party to perform for, no crowd to defer to, nowhere for a hard truth to be diluted into consensus. It is also the only unit with enough bandwidth for real depth: you cannot hold five people to their deepest commitments with the attention you can give to one.

This is the design that lets the practice be both intimate and far-reaching. The *network* can spread without limit; each *node* stays small and deep. That is something no institution has ever managed, because the depth is exactly the part that does not scale — so we do not try to scale the depth. We let it multiply, one pair at a time.

---

## The practice

A few commitments define a Call relationship. They are few on purpose — specific enough to follow, simple enough to carry to someone else.

1. **Write your own creed.** Not adopt a shared one — write your own. A plain statement of what you believe, what you are for, and what you are trying to become. It stays a living document; you revise it as you grow.

2. **Pursue God together, out loud.** Talk about the pursuit of God and how it bears on your real life and the events of the day. God is the center because God is the one goal that outlives every circumstance — unreachable, so the pursuit never ends and never expires. There is an answer; we cannot know it completely; we can always do better.

3. **Hold yourself to your creed, and invite the other to.** Be unvarnished, and receive the other's honesty as a gift rather than a threat. Full resistance, permanent membership — you push on each other freely, and none of it touches whether you stay.

4. **When the relationship grows deep enough, swap creeds.** The most intimate exchange comes last, as the fruit of earned trust — never as the mechanism for manufacturing it. Depth is the precondition, not the goal.

---

## How it spreads

By contagion, never by quota. No one is required to recruit; that is the machinery of a pyramid, and people can smell it. Instead, the practice is built so that being in a good Call relationship naturally makes you want to offer it to someone. If a few people each tend a few pairs, and those pairs quietly seed their own, there is more than enough room for it to spread — without a center anyone could capture, own, or corrupt.

The pairs form among people already in your life with some shared seriousness — a reconnected old friend, a collaborator, someone who mentioned the same longing you feel. Finding them is slow and takes real searching; that difficulty is a feature, not a flaw. It means you arrive ready for depth rather than shopping for it.

---

## What it is not

It is **not a brand**, and its plainness is deliberate — a thing people *do*, not a thing people *join*.

It is **not a single official creed** enforced on everyone. The content stays radically plural, and what is shared across the network is not *what* people believe but *that* they have articulated it and submitted it to one other person's loving scrutiny.

It is **not a program with a finish line.** No one graduates from The Call; there is no curriculum to complete, no level to reach. It is an ongoing relationship in an unending pursuit — the way friendship is, the way prayer is.

---

## Why it lasts

A practice built around a crisis ends when the crisis does. A practice built around a reachable goal expires the moment the goal is met or missed.

The Call is built around the one thing that is never finished: God, approached and never arrived at. Whatever good work the moment asks of us — whatever ark needs building — becomes the meaningful expression of that pursuit, not its purpose. The work fits the times. The pursuit outlives them.
